# Framingham Heart Study: Predicting Total Cholesterol

A statistics project analyzing cardiovascular health data to explore what factors are associated with total cholesterol.

## Dataset

We used the Framingham Heart Study dataset, sourced from Kaggle (originally NHLBI/BioLINCC), containing 4,240 observations across 16 variables covering demographics, lifestyle factors (like smoking and cigarettes per day), and clinical measurements (age, BMI, heart rate, blood pressure, glucose, and total cholesterol).

Our main research question: what factors are associated with an individual's total cholesterol level, and can we build a model that meaningfully predicts it?

## What's in this repo

- `data/` - the raw Assignment 2 dataset
- `assignment-02-dataset/` - dataset selection and variable of interest
- `assignment-03-descriptive-stats/` - data cleaning and descriptive statistics
- `assignment-04-probability/` - probability distributions and assumptions
- `assignment-05-inference/` - hypothesis tests and confidence intervals
- `assignment-06-regression/` - final regression model
- `DECISIONS.md` - a dated log of the key analytical decisions made throughout the project
